About

I am a research assistant in the Physics Department at HKUST, working in Prof. Yilong Han's group. My work sits at the intersection of field theory and pattern formation in colloidal systems, together with the numerical methods that support them. I build models that capture the macroscopic features of collective behaviour and pattern formation, and I develop simulation and analysis pipelines that turn large numerical datasets into quantitative, testable statements in support of theory.

I currently have three projects in their final stages, on the universality of the Gaussian spectrum for phase-space networks of local operators, on couplings and rotational correlations of hexatic colloids from the Langevin equation, and on a reaction-diffusion model of hyperbolic patterns in biological systems. I am preparing applications for doctoral study starting in autumn 2027, and I am glad to discuss my work in more detail by email.

Research interests

  • Soft matter and soft condensed matter physics — the collective behaviour and classical many-body interactions of such systems, studied through theory, simulation, and data analysis.
  • Modelling with mean-field theory and finer-grained field theories; phase transitions studied through the free energy.
  • Introductory differential-geometry methods for pattern formation in biological systems.
