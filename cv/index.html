Research Experience

  • Research Assistant — Yilong Han Lab

    Sep 2026 – Aug 2027

    Department of Physics, The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Hong Kong SAR.

    • Proved that phase-space networks for systems with local correlations admit a universal Gaussian spectrum.
    • Computed and simulated Langevin dynamics for the coupling of hexatic and glassy phases in two-dimensional colloidal disks, in collaboration with experimentalists; found that correlations oscillate in these systems.

  • M.Math Part III Essay — Pattern formation in mixtures of active and passive particles

    Mar 2026 – May 2026

    Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics (DAMTP), University of Cambridge. Supervisor: Prof. Robert Jack.

    • Investigated the breakdown of the equilibrium spinodal–binodal relationship in active matter, building on the reciprocal Cahn–Hilliard model, its non-equilibrium extensions, and the active–passive lattice gas.
    • Showed that a generalised free energy enforces a one-to-one correspondence between the stationary spinodal and binodal in scalar and reducible multicomponent systems, and identified parameter regimes in which linear instability persists outside global phase separation.

  • Research Assistant — Generative models for inverse problems in string theory

    Jul 2025 – Sep 2025

    DAMTP, University of Cambridge. Supervisor: Prof. Krippendorf.

    • Adapted methods from image generation to reconstruct eight-dimensional flux vectors in string theory flux vacua, implementing and fine-tuning latent diffusion models (LDM) and continuous conditional diffusion models (CCDM) in PyTorch.
    • Worked on model design, hyperparameter optimisation, and the preprocessing of high-dimensional discrete data.

  • Research project — Theory of the structural colour of Siler collingwoodi

    Jul 2023 – Aug 2024

    With Huize Gao, Zhang Wei Lab, School of Life Sciences, Peking University.

    • Identified systematic errors in classical optical models of chitin layers through simulation and theoretical analysis, and validated observational data by wavefront and mode analysis.

  • Summer school participant

    Jul 2023 – Aug 2023

    Institute of Physics, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). International Conference on Physics under Synergetic Extreme Conditions.

    • Gained exposure to high-pressure (diamond anvil cells) and ultra-low-temperature (10 mK) experimental techniques for quantum materials.
